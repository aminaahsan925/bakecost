Questions

Do I need to install anything?

No. It runs in your browser, on your phone or computer. The guest demo needs no account.

What happens to my invoices?

In guest mode they never leave your browser. Signed in, they live in your account. Audit invoices are used only for your audit, and deleted on request.

My invoices are paper photos. Is that fine?

Yes. Photos and PDFs both work. Even one invoice is enough to start.
