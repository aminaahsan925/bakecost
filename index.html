Why BakeCost exists

Bakers price from memory. Prices do not sit still.

In one year, flour rose 24%, eggs fell 35% and butter fell 17%. Those are US Bureau of Labor Statistics figures, September 2026. A recipe priced last year is a guess today.

BakeCost was built in Lahore in November 2026 by Amina Ahsan and Hadi Kashif. It reads the invoices you already get, builds a price history for every ingredient, and recosts every recipe. No typing prices in by hand.

The numbers move. The recipes should move with them.
