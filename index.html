Ingredient prices move. Your recipe costs should move with them.

A baker prices a cake from what butter, flour, eggs and sugar cost when she last checked. But those prices move every few months, in both directions, and nobody tells her. A cake costed with last year’s prices is priced wrong today, in one direction or the other.

One bakery pricing guide puts it plainly: recost your best sellers every quarter, and any time an ingredient moves more than 10 percent. Almost no small baker does this, because recosting means finding old invoices, retyping prices, and redoing the maths for every recipe. That is the part BakeCost does for you.

Everything in one place

The app does the whole loop, from the invoice in your hand to the margin on each recipe.

1

Invoice intake, your way

Upload a photo or PDF, paste the invoice text, or type the lines by hand. Every line is shown in an editable preview before it is saved, so you check the numbers first.

2

Price history per ingredient

Every ingredient gets a price line over time, with a chart, so you can see exactly what moved, when, and by how much. Grams convert to kg automatically.

3

Recipes recosted automatically

Add a recipe once, with its ingredients and selling price. Every new invoice recosts it from the latest prices, and shows the cost then and now.

4

Margin alerts

Set a margin target per recipe. Green means on target, amber means close, red means below. The alert feed also flags any ingredient that moved 5% or more.

5

Dashboard and biggest movers

Ingredients tracked, recipes, active alerts, and the biggest price move, on one screen, with a recent activity log of everything you added.

6

Export any time

Download your recipes, price history, or invoices as CSV files, ready to open in a spreadsheet. Your data is yours to take with you.

Questions

Do I need to install anything?

No. BakeCost runs in your browser, on your phone or computer. There is nothing to install. The guest demo needs no account at all; sign in with Google only if you want your data saved across devices.

What do you do with my invoices?

In guest demo mode, your invoices stay in your browser and are never sent anywhere. With a signed-in account, they are stored in your account so they follow you across devices. Invoices sent for the free audit are used only for your audit, they are not shared with anyone, and we delete them whenever you ask. You can redact anything you like before sending, as long as the ingredient names, quantities and prices stay readable.

My invoices are paper, or photos on my phone. Is that fine?

Yes. Photos or PDFs both work. Even one invoice is enough to start. In the app, photos and PDFs are read by AI; pasted text and typed lines work without AI.

What does the monthly plan include?

The app, with your recipe limit, plus margin alerts and CSV export. Each month you add your new invoices and your recipes recost themselves from the latest prices. The Bakery plan also includes a monthly “what changed” report showing every ingredient that moved and what it did to your margins.

What if a price on an invoice is read wrong?

Every invoice is shown in an editable preview before it is saved, so you check and fix each line first. You can also delete an invoice any time, and its prices leave the price history.
