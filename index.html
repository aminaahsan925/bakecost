Ingredient prices move. Your recipe costs should move with them.

A baker prices a cake from what butter, flour, eggs and sugar cost when she last checked. But those prices move every few months, in both directions, and nobody tells her. A cake costed with last year’s prices is priced wrong today, in one direction or the other.

One bakery pricing guide puts it plainly: recost your best sellers every quarter, and any time an ingredient moves more than 10 percent. Almost no small baker does this, because recosting means finding old invoices, retyping prices, and redoing the maths for every recipe. That is the part BakeCost does for you.

How it works

Three steps, from documents you already have. No POS, no inventory system, no new software to learn.

Step 1

Send your supplier invoices

Photos or PDFs of your last 3 months of supplier invoices, by email. Even one invoice is a start.

Step 2

We build your price history

Every ingredient gets a price line over time, so you can see exactly what moved, when, and by how much.

Step 3

See margins per recipe, plus alerts

Every recipe is recosted from the latest prices. Set a margin target, and get an alert when any recipe drops below it.

Questions

Do I need to install anything?

No. The audit is done by hand from the invoices you email us. There is nothing to install and nothing to sign up for. The monthly plans work the same way: you send your invoices each month, we send your recosted recipes and alerts back.

What do you do with my invoices?

They are used only for your audit. They are not shared with anyone, and we delete them whenever you ask. You can redact anything you like before sending, as long as the ingredient names, quantities and prices stay readable.

My invoices are paper, or photos on my phone. Is that fine?

Yes. Photos or PDFs both work. Even one invoice is enough to start.

What does the monthly plan include?

Each month we process your new invoices, recost your recipes from the latest prices, and send you margin alerts for any recipe below your target, plus a short “what changed” report showing every ingredient that moved and what it did to your margins.
